Apostolic Actions

Pilgrim Mary Visits

Bringing Our Lady's statue into homes to foster family prayers.

Homebound Communion

Bringing the Holy Eucharist to parishioners unable to attend Mass.

Book Borrow Outreach

Sharing Catholic literature and sacramental items in public areas.

Rosary Making

Hand-crafting rosaries for distribution in local parish events.

Prison Ministry

Bringing hope and spiritual guidance to youth in local facilities.
