Our Mission

We are a lay Catholic organization dedicated to glorifying God through the sanctification of our members, developed by prayer and active co-operation in Mary’s and the Church’s work of crushing the head of the serpent and advancing the reign of Christ.

Membership Ranks

  • Active Members: Commit to attending weekly meetings and performing a minimum of two hours of active apostolic fieldwork under clerical guidance.
  • Auxiliary Members: Provide vital spiritual supply lines by reciting the Rosary and the Legion prayers (Tessera) daily from home.

Apostolic Works

  • Pilgrim Mary Visitations: Bringing a beautiful statue of Our Lady into parish homes to foster family prayers and consecrations.
  • Communion to Homebound: Serving as a living bridge between the parish church and members unable to attend Mass.
  • Book Borrow Evangelization: Distributing Catholic books, sacramentals, and rosary guides in public places.
  • Rosary Making: Crafting hand-made rosaries to distribute during our outreach campaigns and deployments.
  • Prison Ministry: Bringing spiritual guidance and hope to youth incarcerated in local juvenile facilities.
