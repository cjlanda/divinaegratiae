Apostolic Works

  • Pilgrim Mary Visits Visitations to parish homes to pray the Rosary.
  • Homebound Communion Bringing the Eucharist to sick and elderly parishioners.
  • Book Borrow Outreach Spreading Catholic literature in local public areas.
  • Rosary Making Crafting rosaries for distribution and missions.
  • Prison Ministry Bringing hope and spiritual guidance to youth.

Awaiting Volunteers

  • Visiting the Dying Comforting individuals in their final hours and preparing them.
  • Reaching Lapsed Catholics Welcoming former parishioners back to sacraments.
  • Outreach to the Poor Combining material aid with genuine companionship.

Membership Paths

Active Members

Attend weekly assemblies and perform 2 hours of active parish service under spiritual guidance.

Auxiliary Members

Recite the daily Rosary and the Legion prayers from home, supplying vital spiritual strength.
